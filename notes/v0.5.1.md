Fixes 0.5.0 not starting on some Windows PCs ("WinError 10013 ... forbidden by its access permissions").

Windows (Hyper-V / WSL) can reserve whole port ranges, sometimes including the dashboard's port 8780. The
dashboard now moves to a free port when that happens (18780 first), remembers it for next time, and opens your
browser on it. The console window shows the address it uses.

If you sync layouts to the head unit from this PC, use the address shown in the console in the head unit's
settings.

Install over 0.5.0 / 0.4.x (same signing key); your drives, pairings and settings are kept.
