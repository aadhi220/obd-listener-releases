A redesigned PC dashboard with new pages, purpose-built layouts, and a refreshed Android app.

**PC dashboard (double-click the exe, then use the sidebar):**
- **Drives:** every recording, with length, distance, top speed, O2 sensor activity, fault-mode share, stalls and
  marks. Search, filter, **Play**, or open one for its charts, a CSV download or removal (moved to a trash folder).
- **Diagnostics:** trouble codes with plain descriptions, the check-engine light, readiness monitors, the freeze
  frame and the engine computer's own test results. Clearing codes asks first.
- **Car health:** patterns across all your drives: O2 sensor dropouts by day and by speed, the moment the signal
  drops out, stalls, idle quality and fuel trims. Prints as a report for a mechanic.
- **Connection:** pick the adapter type, **Scan** for Bluetooth adapters or list COM ports, and connect. The
  dashboard reconnects to it next time.
- **Phone sync** and **Settings & about** (light / dark / system colours).
- **Editing during a replay** shows which values the recording has, so you only add widgets with data.

**Purpose-built layouts** replace the old ones. **Drive** is the everyday default; the others each show everything
needed to understand one problem, with charts on the same timeline: Hesitation & stalling, O2 sensor test, Rough
idle, Lean / rich mixture, Throttle & pedal sensors, Overheating, Battery & charging and Fuel economy. Each comes
in a PC size and a head-unit size, and the **ⓘ** button explains how to read it. Layouts you made are kept.

**Android:** the phone screen and the head-unit panels follow the same design, and the phone follows the system
light/dark setting. A fresh install asks you to choose your adapter (tap the dot, then **Scan**).

**Removed:** the engine simulator and the `sim` command. Use **Drives → Play** to try the dashboard without a car.

Install over 0.4.x (same signing key); your drives, pairings and settings are kept. A head unit set to one of the
old layouts switches to **Drive**.
