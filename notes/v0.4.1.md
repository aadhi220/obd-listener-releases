Fixes found in real drives recorded on the head unit and synced to a PC.

- **One drive, one recording:** if the adapter drops out briefly, recording continues in the same file once the
  car answers again (within 2 minutes). Before, a single engine run could be split into many short files.
- **No more impossible readings:**
  - The cheap BLE adapters sometimes lose a character or a whole frame of a reply. That used to shift every
    value after the gap, which produced readings like fuel trim −94 %, 32 V or a speed spike.
  - Damaged replies are now thrown away instead of decoded, in both the Android app and the Windows tools.

Install over 0.4.0 (same signing key); your drives, pairings and settings are kept.
