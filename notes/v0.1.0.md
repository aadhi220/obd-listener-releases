First public release.

**Windows: `obd-listener-0.1.0-windows-x64.exe`**

- **Double-click for the demo:** a built-in engine and ELM327 simulator (with engine sound and fault injection)
  plus the dashboard, opened in your browser.
- **`dash`:** configurable web dashboard with drag-and-drop layouts and live data from BLE, Bluetooth Classic,
  USB or Wi-Fi ELM327 adapters. It records, replays, and reads and clears trouble codes (with confirmation).
- **`sim`:** the simulator on its own, as a Wi-Fi-style adapter on `127.0.0.1:35000`.
- **`listen`:** adapter tools: `scan`, `probe` (writes a report of what your adapter and car support), `stream`,
  `sniff`, `o2diag`, `term`.
- **Data:** everything is saved in `Documents\OBD Listener`.

**Android: `obd-dash-0.1.0.apk`** (Android 5.0+)

- **Head unit mode:**
  - native dashboard using the same layouts as the PC, connected straight to the BLE adapter;
  - automatic drive recording with a MARK button;
  - a configurable dead strip at the bottom of the screen.
- **Phone mode:** pairs with a head unit by QR code. Drive sync comes later.

Verify downloads with `SHA256SUMS.txt`. The exe isn't code-signed, so SmartScreen may ask: More info → Run anyway.
