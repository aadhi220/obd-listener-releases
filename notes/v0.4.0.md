**New: the whole chain, head unit → phone → laptop, with no server.** **New: the phone is a full OBD app too.**

**Windows (`obd-listener-0.4.0-windows-x64.exe`)**

- **⇄ Sync → Pair a phone** shows a QR code. Scan it with the phone app (Phone mode).
- **At home:** open the app on the phone while it's on the same Wi-Fi as the PC. Drives it collected from the head
  unit, and drives it recorded itself, arrive in `Documents\OBD Listener\reports\headunit\`, ready for ▶ Replay.
- **Firewall:** Windows may ask whether OBD Listener may use private networks. Allow it, or the phone can't reach
  the PC.
- **When it listens:** only while the dashboard is open and a phone is paired. Only the sync port is opened to the
  network; the dashboard stays on this PC.
- **Checked uploads:** every upload is signed with the pairing key and checked against its fingerprint
  (SHA-256).
- **Busy ports:** if Windows has reserved port 8790, a free port is picked and remembered. A port that's already
  in use now gives a clear error instead of silently doing nothing.

**Android (`obd-dash-0.4.0.apk`)**, Phone mode, **Your car**:

- **Live dashboard:** full screen, connects to the OBD adapter, records drives. Use it when the head unit isn't
  connected to the adapter; it takes one connection.
- **Replay a drive:** the timeline replay over the phone's own recordings and the drives collected from the head
  unit.
- **Trouble codes:** read stored and pending codes. Clearing always asks first.
- **Sync:** after collecting from the head unit, the phone sends everything new to a paired laptop, and shows how
  many drives the laptop has confirmed.
- **Installing:** installs over earlier versions without uninstalling.
