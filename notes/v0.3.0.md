**New: copy drives from the head unit to your phone over Wi-Fi.** No cables, no server. Pair once with a QR code,
then the phone collects new drives whenever you open the app in the car.

**Android (`obd-dash-0.3.0.apk`)**: install it on both the head unit and the phone (pick the mode on first launch).

- **Head unit mode** listens for the paired phone while the app is running, including when Open Head Unit is in
  front.
  - **Settings → Phone sync** shows its address and each phone's last sync.
  - **Pair a phone** shows the QR code.
- **Phone mode:** when you open the app, it finds the head unit on its hotspot and copies drives it doesn't have
  yet.
  - **Finding it:** the last address, then the network name, then a quick scan of the hotspot.
  - **Storage:** drives go to `Android/data/com.obdl.dash/files/drives/` on the phone.
  - **Safety:** interrupted copies resume, and each file is checked against the head unit's fingerprint
    (SHA-256) before it's kept.
- **Pairing:** point the phone's normal camera at the head unit's QR code and tap the link. If the camera only
  shows text, copy it and use **Paste pairing link** in the app.
- **Security:** every request is signed with the pairing key. Unknown devices and replayed requests are refused.
- **If the phone says "not found":** check the head unit is connected to the phone's hotspot. Some hotspots stop
  connected devices from seeing each other.
- **Installing:** installs over 0.1.0 and 0.2.0 without uninstalling (same signing key).

**Windows (`obd-listener-0.3.0-windows-x64.exe`)**: same features as 0.2.0. Copying drives from the phone to the
laptop comes in a later version.
