**New: replay with a timeline**: play back a recorded drive to show someone exactly what happened, and skip
straight to the part that matters.

- **Timeline:** shows the drive's speed and RPM, with MARK presses as red lines.
- **Seek:** tap or drag the timeline, ±10 s, or jump to the next MARK (starts 5 s before it).
- **Play/pause and speed:** ¼× to 16× on Windows, ½× to 8× on the head unit.
- **After a jump, the dashboard shows that exact moment:** gauges, chart history and calculated values (gear,
  fuel rate, "O2 signal dead") are rebuilt, not left empty.
- **Charts** follow the recording's own clock, so they show the real time window at any speed.

**Windows (`obd-listener-0.2.0-windows-x64.exe`)**

- **▶ Replay** button, or `dash --replay <file>`: works with dashboard recordings, head-unit uploads and Car
  Scanner exports.
- **Keys:** Space play/pause, `[` `]` ±10 s, `M` next MARK.

**Android (`obd-dash-0.2.0.apk`)**

- **Settings → Replay a drive** plays recordings stored on the head unit. The replay bar stays in the usable top
  area, and ✕ returns to live data.
- Live polling and recording pause during a replay, and replays are never recorded as new drives.
- **Installing:** installs over 0.1.0 without uninstalling (same signing key).

**Fixes**

- The check-engine lamp could be wrong when replaying head-unit recordings (a status field was read as a number).
- Switching from live data to a replay no longer shows a false "error" status.
